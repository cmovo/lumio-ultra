The hidden cost of sourcing

Most agencies run the same sourcing process for every activation. A key challenge is the unpredictable cost of the process.

The standard approach

A brief arrives. A supplier is found, vetted, and introduced to the client's brand. The activation runs. The supplier delivers — or doesn't. The relationship ends there.

The next brief starts from zero.

The hidden cost

What doesn't carry between bookings is anything the agency learned. Which setups worked. What outputs performed. What the brand team cared about most. The supplier changes.

The standards reset. The knowledge that should be compounding across activations, across clients, across a full year's calendar — doesn't.

Three consistent costs

Across a full activation calendar, at least one of these three failure points will appear without exception.

01

The risk

When a spot-hire supplier falls short on the day — late, under-spec, or not where they said they'd be — there's no backup, no escalation path, no system to absorb it. The agency carries the exposure. The client sees the result.

02

The reset loop

A new supplier means a new brand handover. Assets re-sent. Standards re-explained. Interpreted differently by someone who has never worked with this client before — and won't be there for the next one either.

03

The overhead

Sourcing, approving, briefing, and chasing a content capture supplier lands on your producers on every booking. It isn't billed. It isn't recovered. Across a full activation calendar, it's one of the largest hidden costs in the operation.
