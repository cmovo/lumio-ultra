For Agencies

Control your variables.

lumio gives agencies better control over the content capture element — the one part of every activation that's been managed around rather than owned.

Content capture sits inside every activation your agency runs. But sourcing it, briefing it, and guaranteeing it lands to standard — that's a variable that moves with every brief. The cost shifts. The quality varies. The experience depends on who you've managed to book.

lumio removes the variable. The same system, the same standard, the same people — across every activation, across every client.

Risk variables land differently depending on where you sit inside the agency. Here are three perspectives, and what lumio does about each.

Every activation includes a content capture element. Every time, the agency sources it, negotiates it, and absorbs whatever the market gives back — a different rate, a different operator, a different outcome. There's no fixed base cost to plan against, no margin you can protect in advance, and no supplier relationship that carries forward.

The result is a line item that moves. And a line item that moves is one you can't sell confidently, scale cleanly, or account for with any precision.

lumio replaces the variable with a fixed base cost — one that covers every activation in the calendar. The margin becomes predictable. The brief becomes easier to price. And the supplier conversation happens once, not fifty times a year.

A delivery lead's job is to remove uncertainty before event day. Venue confirmed. Logistics locked. Team briefed. But the content capture element carries a risk the others don't — whether you're working with a spot-hire or a brief-by-brief arrangement with a regular partner, no record of your account travels with them. Your standards, your preferences, your history — none of it is formally held anywhere. Each activation begins the same conversation again.

That's not a supplier failure. It's what happens without a platform behind the relationship.

lumio closes that gap. The same operators, briefed against your account standards, with everything your account has built held in the system before they arrive. The activation runs as written — every time, not most of the time.

The client doesn't see the logistics. They don't see the sourcing decisions or the margin calculations. What they see is the operator standing in front of their attendees — how they present, how they engage, how they represent the work your agency has put into the activation.

Whether that operator is a spot-hire or someone you've worked with before on a brief-by-brief basis, the gap between your standard and their delivery risks being visible. They've read the brief. They haven't absorbed the account.

lumio operators are an extension of your agency in the room. Briefed to your account, presented to your standard, and accountable to the quality your clients expect — not to a single booking with nothing behind it.

lumio puts content capture deployment under one system, giving agencies better control over the cost, the standard, and the knowledge that builds with every activation.
