The Activation

Shaped to the brief. Held to a standard.

lumio tools seamlessly integrate across every activation, built to engage.

Every activation lumio delivers is configured around the brief — the hardware, the presentation, the content output. What doesn't change is the lumio standard of delivery. The result is a content capture setup that sits seamlessly within the wider activation, producing branded output that never compromises on quality.

Every event delivery runs in three stages

The Setup.

The activation environment is dressed, branded, and staffed to the client brief before anyone arrives — hardware wrapped, team presented, and every visual element integrated within the activation as a seamless whole.

First impressions are the brief. Everything visible — the equipment, the staff, the branded finish — is prepared and positioned before doors open. The client's brand is present in the room before a single interaction happens, with the setup tested and output optimised within the context of the space.

The Experience.

The capture technology brought to every activation is designed and configured to engage attendees and produce content worth sharing beyond the room.

The tools are configured to the environment and the brief — calibrated to perform within it. The setup commands attention, drawing guests in, and creates an interaction compelling enough to drive immediate and instinctive sharing.

The Output.

Every asset captured is formatted and delivered to the agency post-event, with the activation logged and recorded to the account.

Nothing arrives unfinished. Assets are organised, formatted, and ready for the agency to use without internal processing. The activation is logged — brief, delivery, output — building a record that informs every engagement that follows.
